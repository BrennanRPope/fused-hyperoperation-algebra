Mult-add gets you to the door of general relativity, not through it

Mult-add gives the local rules of motion: the dial q sets the speed of light and the signature, and twist = curvature × area. Einstein's equations also need a rule for how matter bends spacetime. Three routes show exactly where that rule has to come in.

Route a: let the dial vary from place to place

A dial field k(x) = 1/c(x)² changes the local speed of light. On its own it touches only clocks, or clocks and rulers equally (the conformal case). That is a scalar theory of gravity, the kind Einstein tried in 1911–12 and Nordström in 1913.

Exact geodesic integration at the Sun's limb and for Mercury gives the numbers on the right. Every dial-only theory bends light by exactly half, whatever its second-order term.

Stops here. Space has to curve as much as time slows, and the dial alone can't do that. Cassini measured γ − 1 = (2.1 ± 2.3)×10⁻⁵, which puts γ = 0 about 43,000 σ away.

γ measures how much space curves; β how gravity adds to itself. Bending is (1+γ)/2 of GR's, perihelion (2+2γ−β)/3.

Route c: the river of free fall

Two postulates in mult-add's own language:

  1. At every event there is a free-fall frame, the river, in which space is flat and special relativity holds, so speeds compose by mult-add on the Doppler-squared ruler.
  2. The river flows past standing observers at Newton's escape speed, v² = 2m/r.

That is enough for the exact Schwarzschild black hole. A standing clock and a standing ruler are both boosted through the river, and one boost gives both factors: the clock runs at 1 − v² and the ruler stretches by 1/(1 − v²). So the time deficit and the space deficit are mult-add inverses, (−gtt − 1) ✳ (grr − 1) = 0. That is why light bends twice the Newtonian amount: half from clocks, half from rulers, because a boost dilates time and shortens length by the same factor.

The horizon r = 2M is where the river reaches light speed, the wall of the velocity law.

What the river gives, exactly

  • Zero Einstein tensor for v² = 2M/r; Schwarzschild after one clock change.
  • Weak field: β = 1 and γ = 1, so 1.751″ bending, 42.98″/century perihelion, Shapiro delay, redshift.
  • With dust: the spherical Einstein equations are Newton's shell theorem m′ = 4πr²ρ, with the mass carried along by the flow.
  • The Hubble river v = −Hr gives Friedmann: H² = 8πρ/3 is Newton's escape speed for a uniform ball.
  • Gravity Probe B's exact factor √(1 − 3M/r) is 1/γ of the gyroscope's speed through the river: −w² = (−vr²) ✳ (−vo²). The earlier twisted-boosts work took Schwarzschild's space curvature as given; the river now supplies it.

Known physics in our language: Painlevé 1921, Gullstrand 1922, Milne–McCrea 1934, Hamilton and Lisle's river model 2008.

Stops at gravitational waves. A passing wave has real tidal curvature, and its transverse part can't be removed by any change of coordinates, so no slicing makes space flat. A river in flat space can't carry the wave's two polarizations, and the data need them: the Hulse–Taylor orbit shrinks at 0.9983 ± 0.0016 of GR's prediction, and LIGO hears the waves directly.

Route b: twist = curvature × area, in spacetime

Carry a vector around a small loop in the time–radius plane outside a mass and it comes back boosted, with rapidity (2M/r³) × area (numerical transport agrees to 0.5%). Curvature in a plane that contains time gives a boost: the twisted-boosts law with q < 0.

The Bianchi identity comes for free (checked on every river). Its contracted form forces the left side of the field equation to be R − ½Rg, and Lovelock's theorem (1971) says that in four dimensions this, plus Λg, is the only second-order local choice.

The dial's boosts close on −q × a rotation and commute at q = 0. Hojman, Kuchař and Teitelboim (1976) proved that if these boosts are made local and must compose the same way along every path, the only second-order dynamics for a spatial metric is Einstein's, with G and Λ left free. The coefficient in their bracket is the sign of q.

Reaches Einstein's equations, once two things are added by hand: a metric field, and the demand for a local second-order law.

A derivation chain worth writing up

Dial boosts → twist rule (ours) → made local → Hojman–Kuchař–Teitelboim → Einstein's equations. Every link is proved, ours or known. The output is general relativity exactly, so it is a clean account, not new physics.

No new prediction

Every route either reproduces general relativity or is ruled out by data. GW170817 also showed gravity travels at light speed to about 10⁻¹⁵, so light and gravity share one dial.

The one place a varying dial goes beyond GR

Letting q vary inside the boost bracket is active research. In loop quantum cosmology the coefficient turns negative above half the critical density, read as a switch to Euclidean signature (Mielczarek, 1606.03271; disputed in 1610.07467). Bojowald and Duque build consistent theories where the coefficient is a free function (2310.06798, 2404.06375).

Worth following as theory if the dial-as-field idea matters to the project. Its effects show only near Planck density or through free functions, so nothing can test it today.

Not tested here

Matter with pressure (it adds a second clock factor to the river), rotating black holes (the river also twists there), and the field equations beyond weak fields outside spherical symmetry.
