  k = 3 (Q = 4): ordered start -1.5310, disordered start -1.4014, gap 0.1296; exact latent heat 0.0000, exact midpoint -1.5000
  k = 7 (Q = 8): ordered start -1.6161, disordered start -1.0829, gap 0.5332; exact latent heat 0.4864, exact midpoint -1.3536

  k = 9 matches the exact latent heat to 0.2%; k = 7 is 10% high at this size. At k = 2 and 3 the transition is continuous

(c) Dial gas on L x L tori: mu(n) = log Z_n - log Z_(n+1), the log fugacity that holds n particles.

  L = 5: k=1: +3.94, k=3: +0.32, k=5.83: -1.09, k=12: -1.99, k=40: -1.81
  L = 6: k=1: +3.98, k=3: +0.46, k=5.83: -0.82, k=12: -1.65, k=40: -2.97
  Pair weight where the middle goes flat: L = 4: k = 3.06, L = 5: k = 3.41, L = 6: k = 3.69; it climbs with size toward k_c = 3 + 2 sqrt 2 = 5.83.
  Past k_c the gas fills at one fixed fugacity while the dense phase grows: a true fill-then-spill in
  density, the same shape as latent heat in energy.

(d) Contrast with the landscape border:
  Latent heat shrinks to nothing as k falls to 3 (an essential zero, faster than any power).
  The border linger grows without limit as d falls to 0 (power -1/2).
  And the edge laws of both models sit at (k, 0) on the landscape, in the two-walls country for every
  k > 0: the physical phase changes happen while the law itself never crosses the border.
