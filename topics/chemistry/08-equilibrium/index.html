Lead check: not a lead

Every result here is exact, and the MWC identity gives the weighted fold a clean biochemical meaning, but MWC, Bjerrum's diacid picture and the real-zeros criterion are all established. Nothing here predicts something those models don't, so I didn't follow it further.

In a diacid the dial measures how much the two charges feel each other

A binding polynomial lists a molecule's states weighted by concentration. For two sites with interaction factor ω it is 1 + K₁h + K₂h + ωK₁K₂h², so P − 1 = (K₁h) ⊞ω (K₂h): the dial of subject 7 again.

Dicarboxylic acids measure ω directly. With two equal sites the gap between the two pKa values is log 4 − log ω, where log 4 = 0.60 is the gap two independent sites would show from counting alone. So ω = 4·10−ΔpKa. From Wikipedia's table it climbs from 0.004 for oxalic acid, where the two acid groups touch, to 0.74 for sebacic acid, eight CH₂ groups apart: from near-exclusion toward independence. It never passes 1, since like charges only repel. This is Bjerrum's 1923 electrostatic picture in dial form.

For two sites, cooperativity begins exactly where the zeros leave the real line

For two equal sites the binding polynomial is 1 + 2x + ωx², and three things change together at ω = 1, the circle operation (called mult-add before 1.0) setting:

  • Hill coefficient: it is 2√ω/(1 + √ω), above 1 exactly when ω > 1.
  • Zeros: they are real for ω ≤ 1 and leave the real line for ω > 1, at distance 1/√ω from 0. Every such pair lies on one circle, the one through 0 and −1.
  • Dial: k = ω passes the circle operation.

Lean proves the no-real-zero half by completing the square. The link between real zeros and Hill coefficients at most 1 is known for binding polynomials in general.

Zeros of 1 + 2x + ωx² as ω runs from 0.1 to 10: two real zeros (blue) meet at x = −1 when ω = 1, then leave the real line along the circle through 0 and −1 (red).

Folding the dial over n sites gives the MWC model with exclusive binding

Folding the dial over n identical sites gives 1 + ((1 + kx)ⁿ − 1)/k. Expanded, a state with j ligands carries C(n, j)·kj−1: only the first ligand pays full price, and every later one is k times more welcome. Multiplied through by k this is (k − 1) + (1 + kx)ⁿ, which is the Monod–Wyman–Changeux model of a protein with a tense state that binds nothing and a relaxed state with n independent sites, with allosteric constant L = k − 1. The saturation curves match exactly (checked for n = 2, 3, 4), and Lean proves the identity.

So the weighted fold is not a new model of cooperativity: it is the exclusive-binding limit of MWC. For four sites its Hill coefficient climbs from 1 at k = 1 to 2.42 at k = 100 and 3.03 at k = 1000, approaching 4 slowly.

Hill coefficient of the dial folded over 2, 3 and 4 sites, against k. Each rises from 1 at the circle operation toward the number of sites.

Pressure pushes nitrogen fixation forward, and non-ideality helps

Mass action multiplies partial pressures into the reaction quotient, so N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ goes as pressure to the minus two. At 700 K with a 1 : 3 feed, the constant-ΔH model from subject 7 gives an ammonia fraction of 0.6% at 1 bar, 29% at 100 bar and 41% at 200 bar. The van der Waals fugacities from subject 6 raise the 200-bar value to 45%. Holding ΔH constant overstates K at 700 K, so these are rough model values, not reactor data.
