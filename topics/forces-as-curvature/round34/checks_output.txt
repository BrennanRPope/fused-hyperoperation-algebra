== G1. The flat lattice and the symmetries of the second-order Regge action
PASS G1 (numbers) spacetime glued from the Freudenthal triangulation of the 4D hypercubic lattice is flat: every joint's deficit, 2 pi minus the angles meeting there, is zero. The second-order Regge action M(k) is Hermitian (Schlafli's identity). Moving the vertices costs nothing (4 exact zero directions at every wavevector, as diffeomorphisms should), and there is exactly one more flat direction at every wavevector. At k = 0 that extra one is the lattice's own flex: a change of edge lengths, the same for every choice of axes, that keeps every piece flat to first order but is not a change of metric.
