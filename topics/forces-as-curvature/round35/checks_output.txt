== E2. How a wave's energy bends spacetime (exact, second order, averaged over a wavelength; G = 1)
PASS E2 (exact sympy; textbook Isaacson 1968) a + and x wave solves Einstein's equations at first order. At second order, averaged over a wavelength, its curvature is exactly -8 pi times Isaacson's energy tensor t = <d h d h>/32 pi, which is the curvature a beam of light-like matter with the wave's energy would make: energy density equal to its flux, no pressure across it, Ricci scalar 0. On a slice, the wave's own ripples and its motion each supply half of that energy, so the smooth slice underneath must bend by 16 pi rho_gw, and the law's local dial (the mean bending, round 30) rises by 8 pi rho_gw / 3: exactly the rule rounds 23 and 25 found for matter.
