# Prior-art audit B: the forces-as-curvature stream (B1–B11)

*Literature check, Nov 27 2026. Verdicts come from web searches plus reading abstracts and pages (listed per row). An empty search never counted as "new".*

## Summary

Every physics result in B1–B8 is already in print, usually more directly than the READMEs say. Two of the project's "ours" items turned out to be published formulas: B2's tide formula (Sussman 2013) and B5's lowest wave (Abbassi et al. 2013 give the exact mode for every n). What is left as the project's own are readings, and a few small exact computations nobody seems to have written down: B5's (3, −6) couplings on S³, B9's product form and Sommerfeld = Thomas equality, and B11's Poisson-clock reading. Each is a one- to few-line consequence of published formulas. None of them is a prediction.
