## Plain read on leads

- **G2 (map functions as a Hamacher/AMH family)** opens a small lead worth a note. The line is almost certainly Felsenstein's family, but nobody has noticed it is the Hamacher t-conorm family on 2r. That gives geneticists the copula toolbox and a rapidity reading of Kosambi. Check Felsenstein's printed combining rule before writing it up.
- **N11 (UDD = Shapley)** and **G5 (a Lee–Yang radius test for pairwise vs concerted binding)** are clean, checkable cross-links with no prior statement found. G5 needs Briggs 1985 read in full first.
- **Run-price period law** looks like a genuine empirical refinement, and it's falsifiable on newer catalogues.
- A76, P153 and P180 are essentially in print and should be relabelled.
