## Leads worth following (on their own merits)
1. **M230.** If the proof holds, MMI for every pinned ferromagnetic Ising free energy is a clean statement with a holographic payoff. Check GHS-inequality papers first, since they are the closest neighbour.
2. **S145.** The CHSH = 2·J(X) pattern could be a general theorem about symmetric state spaces, not just ℓp.
3. **new-ties.** The explicit collision tests fill a gap that Bárány–Verma and Kittle–Kogler leave open (which specific parameters work). That is a genuine contribution.
4. **M189.** Before calling it a prediction, read Zolotarev 1957 and Jurayev 2020. The constant is likely derivable, and may already be printed.
