# Posting the paper on arXiv: what's left (all yours)

The paper is in `main.tex`, with figures in `figures/` and the checks in `anc/checks.py`, `anc/EnergyShift.lean`, `anc/Booking.lean`, `anc/Stability.lean`, `anc/PlanckEinstein.lean`, `anc/NoGap.lean` and `anc/Unseen.lean`. Nothing has been submitted.

1. **Read every line and fill in the blanks.** Add your affiliation (or "Independent researcher") and email, confirm or edit the acknowledgment, and delete the DRAFT line under the title. Under arXiv's 2026 rule, the author answers for every line, including anything an AI tool wrote, and a clearly unchecked AI paper can bring a one-year ban.
2. **Preview the PDF.** It couldn't be compiled here. Upload this folder to Overleaf, or let arXiv build it from source when you submit.
3. **Get an endorsement for quant-ph.** First-time submitters need one from an established arXiv author in that area; an institutional email no longer counts on its own (since January 2026). Start a submission and arXiv gives you an endorsement code to send to one person you choose.
4. **Consider the expert check first.** The expert note drafts (kept in project files, not in the repo) ask whether the energy-zero step is already published. The same person could be your endorser.
5. **Paste the abstract.** arXiv's abstract box takes at most 1,920 characters and no custom macros. The abstract in `main.tex` is 1,908 characters and uses only standard TeX, so it can be pasted as is.
6. **Submit.** Upload `main.tex`, the `figures/` folder, `anc/checks.py`, `anc/EnergyShift.lean`, `anc/Booking.lean`, `anc/Stability.lean`, `anc/PlanckEinstein.lean`, `anc/NoGap.lean` and `anc/Unseen.lean` (arXiv treats files in `anc/` as ancillary files). Primary category quant-ph. You'll choose a license and accept arXiv's terms during submission.
