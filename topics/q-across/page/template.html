One q, five worlds

The gluing number q from the elliptic work is the circle-operation dial moved by one: k = q − 1. With that single substitution, the q-integers of quantum algebra, the loop of the Jones polynomial, Euler's partition product and the Ising ring all become circle-operation statements.

[m + n]q = [m]q ∘q−1 [n]q    and    (1 + a)·qⁿ = 1 + (a ∘₁ (q − 1)·[n]q)

The first line says q-integers add by the circle operation ∘ₖ with dial k (called weighted mult-add before 1.0). The second says that going n times round the torus's q-direction is one circle-operation step of length (q − 1)·[n]q. Both are proved in Lean for every n.

The dial is q − 1

The q-integer is [n]q = 1 + q + q² + … + qn−1. Standard algebra gives [m + n] = [m] + qᵐ[n]. Rewritten with k = q − 1 that rule is exactly the circle operation a + b + k·ab, so [n]q is n copies of 1 under ∘k.

Your four dial settings land on the four famous values of q:

The elliptic gluing joins in through the lift x ↦ 1 + x: multiplying 1 + a by q is combining with q − 1 by the circle operation, so n trips round the torus combine by the circle operation to (q − 1)·[n]q, which is the circle power (q − 1)∘₁n.

Standard: q-integers (Gauss, Heine). In the language of formal group laws, (1 + x)ⁿ − 1 is the known n-series of the multiplicative law. The reading of the four famous q's as your four dial settings is the new arrangement.

Where q sits against the rung flow

τ and q are one rung apart. The elliptic modulus has two coordinates: τ, which glues the plane by translation (rung 0), and q = e2πiτ. Since q − 1 = E(2πiτ) with E(x) = eˣ − 1, the multiplicative coordinate is the additive one moved up exactly one step of the flow. Climbing that step also rolls the plane into a cylinder, because E(z + 2πi) = E(z). So at rung 1 only one gluing is left, circle-operation translation by q − 1, and it closes the cylinder into the same torus. Same torus, two charts. This part is exact.

q-deformation is a separate direction, not a step along the flow. The flow law Bs(a, b) = Es(E−sa + E−sb) expands as

Bs = a + b + s·ab + s(s − 1)/4·(a²b + ab²) + …

while the dial law is a + b + k·ab with nothing after it. So k = s to second order: the dial points along the flow as it leaves addition, and the two meet again at rung 1. In between, the dial is a straight chord and the flow is a curved arc that dips to a cubic term of −1/16 at the half rung. (Exact symbolic algebra to third order, in flow_check.py.)

On the landscape, the q of q-counting runs along the k axis. The elliptic gluing q is a third direction: it opens the node at the circle-operation point into a torus (the elliptic page's picture), crossing both the rung sheet and the dial.

Where it is only an analogy: a torus "at half a rung" is the same torus in another chart, and E½ exists only by regular iteration on the real line (its series diverges, Baker 1958), so fractional-rung gluing is formal. One new definition worth a look, not standard: q-integers along the flow, [n]s = Es(n·E−s(x))/x = n + s·C(n, 2)·x + …, which gives [n]q at s = 1. Nobody has shown it means anything yet.

Knots: the Jones polynomial is a sum over the circle-operation cube

Kauffman's bracket smooths each crossing one of two ways. A diagram with n crossings therefore has 2ⁿ states: the corners of the n-cube, the same cube as the circle-operation corner expansion. Each corner counts A#A − #B times dloops − 1, and the loop value is

d = −A² − A⁻² = −A⁻²·[2]A⁴

So the knot q and the counting q are one q (Jones's t is A⁻⁴). Khovanov homology is built on exactly this cube of states.

Primes and partitions: Euler's product is a circle operation

Euler's product (1 − q)(1 − q²)(1 − q³)⋯ is 1 + (−q ∘₁ −q² ∘₁ −q³ ∘₁ …), by the library's list lift. Its corner expansion is a signed sum over sets of distinct parts. Euler's pentagonal theorem says almost everything cancels:

∏(1 − qⁿ) = 1 − q − q² + q⁵ + q⁷ − q¹² − q¹⁵ + q²² + q²⁶ − …

Franklin's picture shows why: slide the smallest row of a Ferrers diagram onto its staircase edge (or back). That pairs off partitions with an even and odd number of parts, leaving only the pentagonal shapes unpaired.

Theta and four squares

The theta function θ(q) = Σ qk² = 1 + 2q + 2q⁴ + 2q⁹ + … is the function the Tate gluing makes periodic. Jacobi's triple product writes it as ∏(1 − q²ⁿ)(1 + q²ⁿ⁻¹)², another fold of 1 + x factors. And θ⁴ counts the ways to write n as four squares:

θ(q)⁴ = 1 + 8·Σ (sum of the divisors of n not divisible by 4)·qⁿ

This is where primes enter: a prime p has only the divisors 1 and p, so it is a sum of four squares in exactly 8(p + 1) ways.

Counting and physics

Gaussian binomials: the cube with a q on each corner

The q-binomial theorem says (1 + t)(1 + qt)⋯(1 + qn−1t), which is 1 + (t ∘₁ qt ∘₁ … ∘₁ qn−1t), has layer k equal to qk(k−1)/2[n, k]q. Geometrically, [n, k]q counts lattice paths in a k × (n − k) box by the area under them:

That gives [4, 2]q = 1 + q + 2q² + q³ + q⁴. At q = 1 it is 6 (Pascal, the circle-operation cube's layer), at q = 2 it is 35 (the planes in a 4-dimensional space over the two-element field), at q = 0 it is 1, and at q = −1 it is 2 (Pascal folded in half). Lean proves the q = 1 case for every n and k (gauss_at_one).

The quantum group Uq(sl₂)

Its defining relation [E, F] = (K − K⁻¹)/(q − q⁻¹) holds on every irreducible representation because of a single ring identity, [k + 1][n − k] − [k][n − k + 1] = [n − 2k], proved for any commutative ring (uq_sl2_commutator). At q = 1 it becomes the ordinary rule [E, F] = H. Its 2-dimensional representation has quantum dimension q + q⁻¹, minus the knot loop value.

The Ising ring

Spins on a ring with domain-wall weight u have partition function

Z = (1 + u)ⁿ + (1 − u)ⁿ = 2 + u∘₁n + (−u)∘₁n

The two circle powers cancel every odd layer of the cube, which is the statement that a ring always has an even number of walls. Proved in Lean for every n (ising_eigen, ising_trace, ising_circ). The bridge to knots is standard too: the Potts model's state count Q and the knot loop value satisfy Q = d², through the Temperley–Lieb algebra.

What is new and what isn't

Every identity on this page is standard mathematics. The new part is the arrangement: one substitution, k = q − 1, puts the famous q's on your dial (counting at 1, volume at 2, union at 0, parity at −1), makes the Tate gluing a circle-operation step, and shows the knot cube, the partition product and the Ising ring as circle-operation folds. The rung flow gives a clean geometric fact: the q dial is the flow's tangent at addition and its chord to rung 1.

Nothing here is a lead as strong as the KdV solitons. The one open thread is the q-integers along the flow, [n]s, a definition that nobody has studied, which may or may not lead anywhere.
