The circle operation alone gives one fixed curvature; a direction dial gives all of it, and then every bridge reaches Einstein

Nov 25, 2026. Can the circle operation's own rules build curved space, and do four known bridges to Einstein's field equations go through? 65 of 65 checks pass; 22 new Lean theorems.

  • The circle operation's own rules build one fixed curvature. Two boosts close on a twist = d × area. That makes relativity's velocity space, a curved space with a single fixed curvature.
  • A dial that varies only by place works in a 2D world and is blocked in 3D and 4D. Schur's theorem: a dial that is the same in every direction at each point must be the same everywhere. This is the exact step where "its own rules" stop.
  • The dial has to depend on direction, so "add to the circle operation ∘₁ (called mult-add before 1.0)" was the right call. The smallest addition is the square rule of a Clifford (geometric) algebra, eiej + ejei = 2kij. That kij is exactly a metric: a renaming of gij, plus the reason it must be there.
  • k as a Clifford number works in the sandwich form a + b + a·k·b. It is associative for every k and keeps the lift 1 + k(a∘₁b) = (1 + ka)(1 + kb). Used alone, a varying k makes no curvature; with a twist field added it is gauge-theory gravity, which is general relativity, and the river is its natural gauge.
  • Every bridge reaches Einstein's equations once the direction dial is in. The circle operation supplies the most in Jacobson's. None gives a new prediction.

k as a Clifford number: what it adds, and what it doesn't

The front-loaded law a + b + k·a·b is associative only when k is central: scalars, plus the volume element in Cl(3,0); scalars only in spacetime's Cl(1,3). The sandwich law works for every k. A constant k is only a change of coordinates, so the information lives in the square rule, which is the metric. A weight that varies by place, used alone, is pure gauge: its curvature is zero identically. Adding an independent twist field gives gauge-theory gravity (Lasenby, Doran and Gull), whose simplest Schwarzschild gauge is our river.

Is it just gij renamed? As an object, yes. It adds the reason a metric must appear (Schur), a Clifford frame (the square root of the metric, needed for spinors and teleparallel gravity), and the twisted-boost law as a commutator. Can k be a fused number? Yes: weights k = 1 + K compose by the same multivector circle operation, so the structure is self-similar, but self-similarity alone makes no curvature.

Teleparallel: the river is a torsion picture already

Checked on every river, with a general flow v(t, x, y, z): the energy rule is e2(K) = 8πρ, where e2 sums the pairwise products of the river's three stretch rates, the cross-term layer of a three-way the circle operation. Moving mass makes the river swirl (curl curl v = −16πj, an exact gravitomagnetic Ampère law), and only the swirl carries bulk action. The torsion scalar on the river is exactly 2·e2(K), and it differs from the curvature scalar by a boundary term, so the teleparallel equivalent of GR holds. A flat-sliced river still can't carry gravitational waves; the general frame can, and that is GR.

Deser's bootstrap, Brans–Dicke and the sombrero

Deser's route needs a massless spin-2 field. The river is a vector and the place dial a scalar; a river wave has no transverse-traceless part. Self-coupling a scalar gives Nordström's theory (light bending zero), already ruled out. The direction dial's traceless part carries exactly the two polarizations LIGO sees, and with it the bootstrap gives Einstein.

A dial that touches curvature makes Brans–Dicke: γ = (1+ω)/(2+ω). Cassini needs ω above about 40,000. The sombrero's role is to give the dial a mass: above about 10−17 eV it hides from Cassini at full strength, and a Higgs-mass sombrero hides it entirely. The light quintessence dial kept alive by the dial-potential search gets no such shelter and must barely couple. The sombrero does not supply the matter-bends-space rule in any bridge.

Leads, judged on their own merits

  • Direction dial = metric, forced by Schur. Worth writing up as pure math: the circle operation's twisted-boost rule, made local, needs a direction-dependent dial, and that dial is a metric. True and geometric; not new physics.
  • Jacobson's chain in circle-operation language. Worth following: every geometric piece is ours, and the open piece is the area law for entropy. If the circle operation's information-keeping could give entropy proportional to area, that would be a real step; nothing here produces it yet.
  • River = teleparallel frame; energy = stretch cross terms; bulk action = swirl. Exact, and a good picture for spinning black holes (a twisting river). Not a new prediction.
  • River as a physical field (Einstein–aether). Testable, but existing research, and GW170817 already removes most of the room.
  • No new prediction anywhere. Every route with the direction dial gives general relativity exactly; every route without it is ruled out by data.
