== 4. Perpetuities and the pole ==
 perpetuity at 2%: price 1/i = 50.0; duration (1+i)/i = 51.0 = 1/d with d = -( (-)i ) = 0.01961
 perpetuity at 5%: price 1/i = 20.0; duration (1+i)/i = 21.0 = 1/d with d = -( (-)i ) = 0.04762
 Gordon: D0(1+g)/(r-g) = 34.6667; perpetuity at r (-) g = 0.028846: D0/(r(-)g) = 34.6667; summed 4000 years = 34.6667
 so a growing stream is a flat perpetuity at the circle-operation difference of the rates; it blows up at g = r (a pole of order 1)
