# Port notes: FusedAlgebra.Topics.TwistedBoosts

- **Change of carrier (the main judgment call).** The original modelled complex numbers as a
  structure `C` of two rationals with hand-written `add`, `mul`, `conj`, `smul`, `nsq`, and 2×2
  matrices as a four-field structure `M`. The port uses mathlib's `ℂ`, `Complex.normSq`, `conj`
  and `Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) ℂ`, with the dial `q : ℝ`. Every original statement is the special
  case of rational coordinates (ℚ(i) ⊂ ℂ and all statements are identities or inequalities in
  the coordinates), so nothing is weakened, but the statements are no longer literally about
  `ℚ × ℚ`. A fully general version (any commutative ring with a quadratic extension, e.g.
  `QuadraticAlgebra`) was judged not worth the cost.
- `oplus` and `twist` are now defined with complex division (`(u + v) / den`, `conj den / den`).
  `oplus_eq_smul` and `twist_eq_smul` prove these agree with the original division-free
  formulas, unconditionally.
- `cosT`, `sinT`, `oplus`, `twist` are `noncomputable` (real and complex division).
- New statements, not in the original: `boost_real_eq_map` (link to `Relativity.boost`),
  `den_eq`, `normSq_den`, `den_ne_zero`, `norm_twist` (`‖twist‖ = 1`), `shapeSubmonoid` and
  `shape_mem_shapeSubmonoid` (the shape is a submonoid of the matrices: the "one group per dial"
  remark, as far as it is cheap; it is a monoid, the group needs `sdet ≠ 0`), `det_shape`
  (`sdet` is `Matrix.det`). `sdet_mul` is now a corollary of `Matrix.det_mul`.
- `twist_half_angle` lost its second hypothesis (`X * X + Y * Y ≠ 0`), which follows from
  `X ≠ 0` over an ordered field.
- `entry_tl`, `entry_br`, `shape_mul` write `1 - q * z` and `α * γ - q * (β * conj δ)` where the
  original wrote `add one (smul (-q) z)`; same value.
- `C.ext'_iff` and `M.ext'_iff` (auto-generated by `@[ext]`, listed in `theorems.tsv`) map to
  `Complex.ext_iff` and `Matrix.ext_iff`.
- `half_kick` and `half_kick_ruler` are stated in any field.
